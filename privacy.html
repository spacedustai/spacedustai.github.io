1. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you contact us by email, sign up for updates, apply for a playtest, or otherwise communicate with us, we collect the information you choose to provide — such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and infrastructure providers automatically record basic technical information when you visit the Site, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. This information is used to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Site.

Information from our games and Services

If you use one of our games or apps, we may collect information needed to provide and improve it, such as account identifiers, gameplay and progress data, in-game purchases, crash and performance diagnostics, device and platform information, and content you create or submit (including text or voice input to AI-powered features). Individual games may provide additional, game-specific privacy disclosures.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Site and Services;
  • Respond to your inquiries and communicate with you, including sending updates you have requested;
  • Personalize gameplay and power interactive features;
  • Monitor and analyze usage, performance, and trends;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, and security incidents;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. AI in our development & Services

We use AI tools to help create our games, including to generate code, art, audio, and other content. These development tools work with our own design materials and do not require your personal information.

If a game or Service includes features that generate content in real time based on your input (for example, text you type), that input and related context may be processed by us and by trusted third-party AI service providers acting on our behalf. We require these providers to protect your information, to use it only to provide services to us, and not to train their general-purpose models on your personal information unless you are clearly informed. Please do not submit sensitive personal information (such as health, financial, or government ID information) into any such features.

4. How we share information

We share information only as described below:

  • Service providers. With vendors who perform services for us, such as hosting, analytics, customer support, payment processing, and AI model providers, subject to confidentiality obligations.
  • Platforms. With app stores and gaming platforms you use to access our games, as needed to provide the Services.
  • Legal and safety. When we believe in good faith it is required by law or necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Spacedust, our players, or others.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to this Policy's protections.
  • With your consent. Whenever you direct us to share information.

5. Cookies & analytics

This Site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. We or our hosting providers may use strictly necessary technologies and privacy-respecting, aggregated analytics to understand how the Site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Our games may use similar technologies as described in their in-game disclosures.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them without appropriate parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete your personal information;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent;
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

California residents and residents of other U.S. states with comprehensive privacy laws, as well as individuals in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and similar jurisdictions, may exercise these rights by emailing us at support@spacedustai.com. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by applicable law, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. International users

Spacedust is based in the State of Washington, United States. If you access the Services from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other countries whose data protection laws may differ from those in your country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and, where the changes are material, provide additional notice as required by law. Your continued use of the Services after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
