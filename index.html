Process

Human vision. AI velocity.

AI does the heavy lifting; people make the calls. Here's how a Spacedust game comes together.

  1. 01

    Dream it

    Every game starts with a human idea — a mechanic, a mood, a question worth playing. AI can’t supply the spark, so we do.

  2. 02

    Generate it

    AI agents write code and generate art, audio, and content in parallel, turning a concept into a playable build at remarkable speed.

  3. 03

    Direct it

    Designers and artists review, refine, and art-direct every output, so the finished game feels crafted, not generated.

  4. 04

    Ship it

    Automated testing and tight iteration loops let us polish and ship at a pace traditional pipelines simply can’t match.
